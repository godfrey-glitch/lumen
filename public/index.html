Thinking frameworks

Four lenses.
One clear answer.

Each framework is a different way of seeing. The right one depends on your decision — and your gut.

⚖️
Pros & Cons
Map the benefits and the costs. Simple, grounding, and clarifying — especially when you're overwhelmed by options.
⏳
10 / 10 / 10 Rule
How will you feel in 10 minutes, 10 months, 10 years? Time-shifting breaks emotional tunnel vision.
🧭
Values Alignment
Does this decision align with what truly matters to you? Your values know the answer before your brain does.
🛡️
Worst-Case Analysis
Define your worst fear. Then ask: could you survive it? Usually the answer is yes — and that's everything.
